2026-08-27 (THURSDAY): New Passkey-Themed Subdomains Target Numerous Industries

AUTHOR:

Matt Brady

NOTES:

- Unit 42 recently identified a domain (passkeyconnect[.]com) that is likely associated with Com-affiliated threat actors (including Bling Libra and CL-CRI-1116, along with their affiliated extortion brands) based on known fingerprints.
- Based on our analysis of all subdomains observed by date of publication of this report, we identified more than 20 organizations operating in the following industries that could be targeted by vishing attempts based on previously observed activity:
  - Financial Services
  - Healthcare
  - High Technology
  - Hospitality
  - Insurance
  - Manufacturing
  - Non-Profit
  - Pharma & Life Sciences 
  - Real Estate
  - Transportation & Logistics
  - Utilities & Energy
- This is the first time we've encountered the use of a domain by the threat actors that was not newly created. 
- Our analysis of Whois data shows that the domain was re-registered via NiceNIC on August 20, 2026 but initially created on March 5, 2023 using Porkbun as a registrar. 

BACKGROUND:

- This activity appears to significantly overlap with prior reporting by Google and Okta.
  - https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/unc6671-targets-financial-services-and-enterprise-cloud-environments
  - https://www.okta.com/blog/threat-intelligence/behind-the-scenes-of-a-vishing-operation/
  - https://www.okta.com/en-au/blog/threat-intelligence/vishing-actors-target-microsoft-entra-passkey-enrollment-/
- We've also included other domains we believe are likely associated with broader activity linked to Com-affiliated threat actors and their associated extortion brands since Google's report published on August 6, 2026.
- All of these domains were created using NiceNIC as a registrar and CloudFlare for name servers.
