- Full OAuth authorization URL: 
  -- hxxps[:]//accounts.google[.]com/o/oauth2/v2/auth?client_id=
     787141854297-otfqv8p1t7ocpm1sc9r6qr9d45jklfea.apps.googleusercontent[.]com&
     redirect_uri=hxxps[:]//fsaremodeling.com/sw/jvfgher45rvbhdf.html&
     response_type=code&scope=openid email profile&access_type=offline&prompt=none
- Due to the existence of prompt=none parameter, visiting this OAuth URL silently redirects to the compromised page without showing any consent prompt, which automatically redirects to the attacker-controlled _wildcard_.therameniers[.]com domain.

- The redirect domain (fsaremodeling[.]com) was registered on 2026-09-16 
  -- Seems to be a legitimate company with an active Instagram account.
- Redirect chain leads to a CAPTCHA page
  -- Solving CAPTCHA shows a “View Files” phishing lure. 
- Clicking on “View Files” leads to an automated download of a malicious executable. (VT score = 22)
