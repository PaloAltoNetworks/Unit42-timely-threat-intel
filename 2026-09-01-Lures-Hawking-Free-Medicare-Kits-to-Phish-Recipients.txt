2026-09-01 (TUESDAY): MyChart Branded Lures Hawking Free Medicare Kits to Phish Recipients PII and Payment Info

AUTHOR(S):

- Amanda Tanner

NOTES:
- Threat actors frequently abuse or subvert legitimate products for malicious purposes; this does not indicate that the product itself is flawed or compromised. Unit 42 has no evidence of any compromise or vulnerability within Epic's MyChart product related to this campaign.
- A large-scale phishing campaign impersonates the MyChart healthcare portal to deceive users into claiming a fraudulent "Medicare Kit."
- The emails rely on a sense of urgency, fake support ticket responses, and promises of high-value medical rewards in exchange for completing a brief survey.
- The campaign operates primarily out of cloud service providers, utilizing highly randomized alphanumeric sender domains that consistently fail standard email authentication checks.
- To bypass email gateways, the attackers employ Bayesian poisoning via hidden text blocks and misuse legitimate cloud services to host payloads and images. 
- Users who click the embedded tracking links are routed through multiple redirector domains to credential harvesting or survey-scam landing pages designed to steal personal information.
For more information on how to stay safe, visit https://mychart.org/staysafe.

DETAILS:

- The emails use a mix of urgency and reward-based lures, including fake "Medicare Kit" surveys, vague support ticket confirmations (Re: [Ticket#1170323]), and monthly prize giveaways.
