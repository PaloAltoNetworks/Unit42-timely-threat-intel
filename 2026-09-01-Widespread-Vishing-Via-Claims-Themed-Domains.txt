NOTES:

- Unit 42 is actively tracking infrastructure that is likely associated with Com-affiliated threat actors (including Bling Libra and CL-CRI-1116, along with their affiliated extortion brands) based on known fingerprints.

- Based on our analysis of approximately 90 observed domains, we identified organizations operating in the following industries that could be targeted by vishing attempts based on previously observed activity:

- Cryptocurrency
- Financial Services
- Healthcare
- High Technology
- Hospitality
- Insurance
- IT Services
– Manufacturing
– Media & Entertainment
– Pharma & Life Sciences
– Professional & Legal Services
– Real Estate
– Transportation & Logistics
– Wholesale & Retail

- The activity has been ongoing since at least June 2026. All of these domains were created using Dynadot as a registrar and CloudFlare for name servers. 
- Each domain is specifically created for an intended target using one of the following criteria:
   - Domain begins with "claims-" or ends with "-claims" using the .com TLD (example: claims-xyz[.]com or xyz-claims[.]com)
   - Domain uses the .claims TLD (example: xyz[.]claims)
