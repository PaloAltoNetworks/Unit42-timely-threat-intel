AUTHORS:

- Reethika Ramesh, Daiping Liu

NOTES:

- We discovered a growth of typo-squatting and phishing domains related to Brazil betting sites
  -- We started with a list of betting brands previously associated with blocking in Brazil
  -- We found the latest ban led to a increase in lookalike, phishing and mirroring activities
- The domain names observed in this list employ:
  -- Cybersquatting techniques
  -- TLD rotation
  -- Addition of suffixes consisting of strings or numbers
- Interestingly, most domains registered during the week of the ban have parked content\
  -- These are potentially opportunistic registrations
- Similar domains that were registered in July–September, 2026
  -- These have clone portals of betting sites
  -- They also have Google Play-like pages pushing Android APK downloads
- Site advertising "BullsBet Casino" are active from a cluster of related domains
  -- Several of these domains were registered around the date of the ban
  -- They are currently serving gambling landing pages that lure users with safety language 
  -- Clicking on links on these sites redirects users to other pages
  -- The other pages are geo-blocked against locations outside of Brazil
- The site at betano-br-casino[.]com explicitly references the ban 
  -- The site mentions new casino bets are banned 
  -- It tells users that balances are available for withdrawal
